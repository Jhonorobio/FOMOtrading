What this is

Paper Terminal is a browser extension for practising trades with virtual money. It places a practice panel over the Buy/Sell panel on fomo.family and records what you would have bought or sold, priced from live public market data.

Nothing here is real. No wallet is connected, no order reaches any exchange or blockchain, and no money moves. Balances, positions and profit or loss are simulated. Paper Terminal is not a broker, exchange, wallet or financial adviser, and nothing it shows is financial advice.

Paper Terminal is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or connected to fomo.family or its operators. Product names and logos belong to their owners and are used only to describe what this tool works with.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older to use Paper Terminal. You confirm your age once, when you set up your practice account, and that confirmation is stored with the date. If you are under 18, do not use this extension.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the extension, write to us and the account and its data will be deleted.

What is never collected

  • Wallet private keys, seed phrases or recovery phrases. The extension never asks for them and has no ability to sign anything.
  • Your fomo.family login or password.
  • Your browsing history. The extension only runs on fomo.family pages, and only reads the token shown on the page you have open.
  • Payment details. Paper Terminal is free and takes no payments.

Who it is shared with

Your data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising. It is not used to train machine-learning models. It reaches other parties only in these ways:

  • Google — when you sign in, to verify your identity. See Google's own privacy policy.
  • Market data providers — DexScreener, GeckoTerminal and Jupiter receive the token addresses being priced, and the network address of the Paper Terminal server. They do not receive your identity or your trades.
  • Legal requirements — if the law requires disclosure.

Where the server is

The extension talks to the Paper Terminal server address shown in Settings. By default that is a server on your own computer, in which case your practice data never leaves your machine. If you point it at a hosted server, the operator of that server holds the data described above.

How long it is kept

Your account and practice history are kept until you delete them. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days. Deleting your account removes everything immediately, as described under Export & deletion.

Your choices

  • See and export everything, as JSON or CSV, from the Style tab or Settings.
  • Correct your username and picture in Settings.
  • Delete your account and all of its data from Settings, at any time.
  • Withdraw the extension's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask us anything about your data using the contact address below.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, or to complain to a data protection authority. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for handling your data is performance of the agreement between us (running the account you asked for), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Security

Sign-in tokens are stored as hashes, requests are restricted to this extension, and the server refuses connections from other websites. No system is perfect; if you find a security problem, please report it to the contact address rather than posting it publicly.

Storage & cookies

Paper Terminal sets no cookies. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixel and no third-party script in the extension. Because of that there is no cookie banner to click.

It does keep a small amount of data on your own device, all of it needed for the extension to work at all:

Stored itemPurposeCleared when

settingsServer address, panel on/off, discreet mode, buy and sell presets, price display, dust filterYou remove the extension

This storage is what privacy law calls strictly necessary, so it does not require consent. If analytics or any non-essential storage is ever added, this page will change and you will be asked first.

Export & deletion

Export

Open the side panel, go to Style, and download your full history as JSON or your trades as CSV. Settings offers the same download.

Delete your account

Open the side panel, click your profile picture, choose Settings, then Delete account and data. This removes your profile, every practice account, all trades, positions, history and sessions from the server straight away. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

You can also email the address below from your account's email address and ask for deletion. Removing the extension alone does not delete server-side data.
